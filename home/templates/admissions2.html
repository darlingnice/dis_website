How to Apply

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Upload required documents (birth cert, passport photo, last report)
  3. Pay the application processing fee
  4. Schedule and attend an interview or placement test
  5. Await admission decision via email or phone

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Child’s birth certificate
  • Two recent passport photos
  • Previous school report
  • Medical record or immunization card
